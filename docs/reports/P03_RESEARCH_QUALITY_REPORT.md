# P-03 Research Quality Report

**Date:** 9 November 2026 · **Scope:** evidence discipline, source reliability, and — explicitly — what could NOT be verified.

## Evidence discipline (as built and as demonstrated)

1. **Provenance is mandatory.** No evidence record exists without source id, source URL (where applicable), retrieval timestamp, reliability class and classification. Publication dates are stored only when the source states them (fixtures and live pages here state none → freshness "unknown", never back-filled).
2. **Ceilings prevent self-verification.** A single source cannot produce VERIFIED; extracted facts from official sources cap at CORROBORATED and only reach it with a second *independent* group. Owner estimates cap at TENTATIVE; community claims at UNVERIFIED.
3. **Contradictions are surfaced, not averaged.** Majority-cluster logic: corroborated 6.5% Etsy fee stands; the forum's 0.5% claim is CONTRADICTED and excluded from scoring but visible in reports.
4. **Gaps are output.** Every research session and report lists what is missing (demand, purchasing intent, competitors, market size, contradictions) in plain language. "Insufficient verified evidence" appears verbatim in smoke findings.
5. **Attention ≠ intent.** Demand stages derive only from evidence categories; views/search volume can never produce purchasing intent or validation Level 4.
6. **Nothing is invented.** Market size is "cannot be established" in all three staging investigations. Prices in commercial analyses are caller-supplied and labelled (estimate). No statistic, review, URL or date was generated by the system.

## What could NOT be verified (explicit)

- **No live platform fee/royalty terms** for KDP, Etsy, Gumroad or NPCI were verified in this environment (timeouts/403). All fee corroboration in staging rests on labelled fixtures demonstrating the machinery.
- **No real demand, sales, review or market-size data** exists in staging. Validation levels above 1 are demonstrated only in tests with explicitly recorded (test) evidence; the three smoke investigations honestly sit at Level 1.
- **No competitor facts** were collected live; competitor machinery is test-demonstrated.
- **Freshness** of every staging record is "unknown" (no source stated a publication date) — reported as such.
- Sandbox live-fetch reliability is environment-bound (egress proxy); the owner's machine may reach sources this sandbox cannot. The inverse is also possible. Either way the system records outcomes truthfully instead of retrying around access controls.

## Quality verdict

The research *machinery* (provenance, corroboration, contradiction, ceilings, gaps) is verified by 61 tests and a live smoke run. The research *content* in staging is deliberately thin and labelled — P-03's honest output for all three investigations is "REQUIRES MORE RESEARCH", which is the correct output for this evidence base.
