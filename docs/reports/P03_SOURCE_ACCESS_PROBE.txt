Purpose: determine whether genuine market research is possible in this environment
         (P-03 §8 source adapters, §35 example investigations).

METHOD
  Two independent probes were run, because they disagree - and the disagreement
  is itself the finding.

  then closes the connection during the TLS handshake. A connectivity check
  based on TCP connect alone reports 28 usable research sources. There are 2.

CONSEQUENCE FOR P-03
  This is the exact analogue of P-02's "no inference runtime installed".
  The source-adapter framework, evidence pipeline, market/competitor/commercial
  analysis, scoring, validation and reporting must all be BUILT AND TESTED, but
  every real-world acquisition path resolves to a source-access refusal at
  runtime. All verification must use mock adapters and clearly labelled test
  data.

  Per §35: "Do not invent three successful opportunities just to satisfy this
  requirement. If genuine research cannot be completed, explain the technical or
  source-access limitation." That instruction is invoked here. No search volume,
  price, review, market size, transaction volume, URL or publication date will
  be fabricated anywhere in P-03.
