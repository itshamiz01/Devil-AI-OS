# ADR-002 — Controlled Autonomy
**Status:** APPROVED (owner lock, 9 Nov 2026) · **Context:** the system should operate businesses independently, but it handles money, publishing and customer communication. · **Options:** (a) maximum autonomy from day one; (b) approval for everything forever; (c) controlled autonomy with earned operating modes. · **Decision:** (c). Maximum operational independence **within** strict financial, security and legal boundaries. Operating modes per business unit: Simulation → Supervised → Controlled Autonomous (Emergency suspends external execution). Mode promotion is an owner decision; the system must never promote itself (P-12). Permission levels L0–L4 apply, L4 always human. · **Reasons:** (a) contradicts the Constitution's own security and financial sections; (b) makes autonomy meaningless. · **Consequences:** every module must classify its actions by level from P-01 onward; restricted operations (unapproved transfers, borrowing/leverage, legal commitments, security changes, self-authorised elevation) are refused by the deterministic policy layer, not by model judgement.
