## Strategy and Executive Intelligence

**Manager:** Chief Strategy Manager (`DEV-MGR-001`)

**Mission:** Develop organizational strategies, evaluate business opportunities, support executive decisions, and monitor strategic objectives.

**Manager duty:** Owns organizational strategy: turns CEO objectives into strategic roadmaps, evaluates opportunities with evidence, and reports strategic risks and progress to the CEO.

**Employees:**

- `DEV-STR-001` **Strategic Planning Specialist** — Create short-term and long-term strategies; Convert business objectives into strategic roadmaps; Identify organizational priorities; Track strategic execution.
- `DEV-STR-002` **Business Intelligence Analyst** — Analyze organizational performance; Identify business trends; Prepare intelligence reports; Identify opportunities and threats.
- `DEV-STR-003` **Market Intelligence Analyst** — Analyze markets; Identify customer demand; Evaluate industry developments; Prepare market intelligence.
- `DEV-STR-004` **Competitive Intelligence Analyst** — Research competitors; Compare products and positioning; Identify market gaps; Monitor competitive changes using authorized sources.
- `DEV-STR-005` **Opportunity Evaluation Specialist** — Evaluate opportunities; Estimate commercial feasibility; Compare potential business models; Prepare evidence-based recommendations.
- `DEV-STR-006` **Risk Strategy Analyst** — Identify strategic risks; Evaluate dependencies; Analyze business exposure; Recommend risk reduction.
- `DEV-STR-007` **Growth Strategy Specialist** — Develop growth strategies; Identify expansion opportunities; Analyze scaling requirements; Recommend growth experiments.
- `DEV-STR-008` **Decision Intelligence Analyst** — Compare decision alternatives; Evaluate assumptions; Analyze evidence; Prepare decision briefs.
- `DEV-STR-009` **Strategic Research Specialist** — Conduct specialized research; Analyze emerging technologies; Identify relevant industry developments; Maintain research records.
- `DEV-STR-010` **Executive Reporting Specialist** — Prepare executive reports; Summarize departmental performance; Track strategic objectives; Prepare CEO briefings.
